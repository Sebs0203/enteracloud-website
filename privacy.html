Privacy Policy

How we handle information when you visit enteracloud.com or contact our team.

Last updated: June 18, 2026

Enteracloud (“Enteracloud,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) operates enteracloud.com and related pages. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share personal information when you use our website or submit a contact request.

Information we collect

Information you provide. When you use our contact form, we may collect your name, work email, company, service interest, and any message you choose to send.

Information collected automatically. When you browse our site, we collect limited technical and usage information through first-party analytics, including pages viewed, referring URL, UTM campaign parameters, scroll depth, and button or link interactions. We do not use third-party advertising cookies on this website.

Session replay. We may record anonymized session replays to understand how visitors use the site and improve usability. Input fields (including email, phone, and password fields) are masked, and we honor browser Do Not Track settings where applicable.

How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and evaluate potential business relationships
  • Operate, secure, and improve our website and marketing
  • Understand site performance and visitor behavior
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms

Where data is stored

Contact information and website analytics data collected through enteracloud.com are processed and stored in the United States on infrastructure we operate or control.

Legal bases and consent

When you submit our contact form, you agree that we may contact you about your inquiry. You may withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time by emailing us, though we may retain records of prior correspondence as needed for business and legal purposes.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who assist us in hosting, email delivery, or internal operations, subject to confidentiality obligations. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, users, or systems.

Cookies and similar technologies

Our website analytics and session replay tools are first-party and self-hosted. We do not set third-party marketing or retargeting cookies through this site. Your browser may store standard session or local storage data required for site functionality. You can limit collection by enabling Do Not Track in your browser or adjusting your browser settings; some features may still operate using server-side or first-party logs necessary to run the site.

Retention

We retain contact submissions and related correspondence for as long as needed to respond to inquiries, maintain business records, and meet legal or contractual requirements. Analytics and session replay data are retained for a limited period consistent with our internal data retention practices.

Security

We apply administrative, technical, and organizational measures appropriate to the nature of the information we process. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information we hold about you, or to opt out of certain processing. California residents may have additional rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know what personal information we collect and how it is used, and the right to request deletion, subject to applicable exceptions.

We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising as defined under California law.

To exercise your rights, contact us at sales@enteracloud.com. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.

Children

Our website and services are directed to businesses and professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be posted on this page.
